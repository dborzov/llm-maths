Open-weight model family from DeepSeek AI (China). Introduced **MLA** (Multi-head Latent Attention) in DeepSeek-V2 (2024) as a memory-efficient alternative to GQA.

Notable for aggressive use of Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture and strong benchmark performance at low inference cost.
