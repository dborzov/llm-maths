Open-weight model family from Alibaba DAMO Academy. Qwen 3 (2025) covers a wide parameter range from 0.6B to 235B (including MoE variants). Qwen 3.5 is the follow-up series.

Notable for strong multilingual capability, long context support, and competitive performance at smaller parameter counts.
