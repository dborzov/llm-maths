UC Berkeley mathematician. Chaired the IEEE P754 committee that produced the 1985 IEEE 754 floating-point standard — the geometry of computer numbers every CPU and GPU uses today.

Turing Award, 1989. His design choice — logarithmic density of representable numbers — has outsized consequences for LLM quantization four decades later.
