AI researcher; co-founder of OpenAI, former Director of AI at Tesla, now independent. Author of **nanoGPT** — the minimal GPT-2 reference implementation that microGPT (this site's reference) is derived from.

His plain-Python transformer implementations are the canonical pedagogical reference for transformer internals used throughout this site.
